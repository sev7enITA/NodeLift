# About and credits

**NodeLift is conceived and developed by Fabrizio Degni**, an independent project exploring local and distributed AI and the continued usefulness of existing hardware.

[Personal website](https://www.fabriziodegni.com/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fdegni/) · [Project showcase / credits](https://www.fabriziodegni.com/NodeLift/#about)

Development is AI-assisted. Published tests and review records distinguish implementation, observed behavior and unresolved qualification. Model review is not physical-device validation.

The [REA study](https://github.com/sev7enITA/NodeLift/blob/main/research/rea-2026-11-01/ASSESSMENT.md) credits its engineering patterns and records reuse decisions. No REA source is vendored in this update.

## In the app

Open **About & References** from the desktop navigation/footer or companion header. It shows the installed version, author and project links, technical references and licenses. **Check releases on GitHub** opens the official release list for manual comparison; it does not download or install updates automatically. See [About and update discovery](https://github.com/sev7enITA/NodeLift/blob/main/docs/ABOUT_AND_UPDATES.md).
