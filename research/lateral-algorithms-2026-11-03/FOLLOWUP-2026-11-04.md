## Accepted corrections and useful proposals

GLM explicitly withdrew its inferred 0.653 seconds per epoch, the 84-116 ms and 189 ms thresholds, the 5.6-7.8x gap and the 1.63x headroom estimate. It also withdrew the interpretation of the local Mac pilot as remote drafting, a universal forward-call accounting identity, and the assertion that constant shape alone suffices for serial equivalence. Those withdrawals agree with the coordinator's earlier objections. No replacement timing threshold is admitted without phase measurements.

Gemini corrected the one-sided versus two-sided binomial confidence convention. For all-success independent Bernoulli trials, the exact lower endpoints are `alpha ** (1/n)` for a one-sided bound and `(alpha/2) ** (1/n)` for a two-sided interval. At 95%, eight successes give approximately 68.8% and 63.1%, respectively. To reach a 98% lower bound requires at least 149 or 183 successes under those respective conventions. Correlated repeats are not independent trials, and detection on injected errors does not establish general system reliability.

Both recommend intermediate-state diagnostics and treating linear candidate salvage as advisory input to a host-owned verifier. GLM additionally proposes a perfectly predictive synthetic candidate and an always-mismatching candidate as controls. These are useful experimental controls, provided they exercise the real engine path and include orchestration overhead. A pure-function test alone cannot measure inference speed, cancellation races, liveness or physical transport.

Gemini proposes serializing one operator family at a time, such as matrix multiplication or attention, before investing in new kernels. This is a useful diagnostic intervention after locating the earliest difference. It may change fusion, scheduling, layouts and other execution details, so it cannot by itself prove the unique causal operator or general equivalence.

## Claims not adopted

| Claim in the follow-up | Coordinator's conclusion |
|---|---|
| GLM: identical serial A/A controls falsify run-to-run nondeterminism | Too strong. They establish repeat equality for those controls only. Candidate profiles require their own repeated runs to assess run-to-run variation. |
| GLM: the probe excludes prompt/artifact causation | Too broad. Inputs were held fixed, which excludes changed inputs as an explanation for these comparisons. Workload sensitivity and model/runtime interactions remain possible. |
| Gemini: history construction altered KV and crossed the quoted margin | Plausible mechanism, not a directly measured KV cause. Final-logit differences and a width-one current step do not identify the first divergent state or operator. Margins must be read from each profile, not borrowed from Metal. |
| Gemini: operator-family wrapping directly proves the necessary subset | A scoped intervention can narrow hypotheses. Neither a small prefix set nor altered execution geometry proves necessity across workloads. |
| GLM: matching metadata makes the suffix a genuine continuation | It establishes a candidate with matching provenance and overlap under the stated preconditions. Future tokens remain unverified. It does not prove they equal the target continuation. |
| GLM: failure of ideal synthetic candidates falsifies near-term payoff | It can reject the tested implementation's benefit on the tested workload and timing regime. It does not prove all asynchronous implementations or future backends futile. |
| GLM: reject phone KV storage and switch to a fixed-width oracle if attribution fails | These are prioritization suggestions, not demonstrated impossibility or authority to change the output contract. Cache affinity remains earlier priority; storage needs measured comparisons. Failure to isolate a cause does not justify silently replacing serial equivalence. |

The retained review text may contain these overclaims. Neither reviewer agreement nor a corrected response overrides the measured scope.

## Concrete next experiment gates

1. **Repeat the candidate path, then localize state differences.** Use the same frozen input and independent fresh contexts; repeat both candidate and serial controls. Add per-layer/operator comparisons for serial-history/current-width-5 and batched-history/current-width-1. Keep baseline logits per profile. Check that instrumentation itself preserves the original observed output. Report intermediate deviations and uncertainty, not a root-cause claim based only on argmax.
2. **Exercise late-candidate integration with an ideal and an adverse source.** Under one serialized host actor and one live lease, feed instant, delayed and always-mismatching candidates through the actual verifier path. Check epoch changes, cancellation, output limits and cleanup. Measure host progress at arrival, useful suffix length, verification time, overhead and discarded work. An ideal source provides an optimistic engineering control, not a phone benchmark.
3. **Only then compare physical workers.** Repeat the post-fix mobile path with the same target/draft pair against host-only and the best qualified local speculation. Retain failures and energy unknowns. Independent task workers and capacity partitioning remain separate workstreams with their own quality and memory tests.
